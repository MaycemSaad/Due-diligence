With more than 10,000 digital asset tokens in circulation, it may be difficult to discern quality from noise, memetic from consequential and groupthink from adoption. The purpose of this framework is to provide a methodology when assessing exposure to tokens. We define the following six risk pillars as a general representation of our methodology: reputational, strategic, technical, financial, legal and compliance, cybersecurity, and auditability. We apply both technical and nontechnical lenses to this framework primarily to remain party agnostic but also because decentralized finance (De. Fi), in its current state, is both technologically sophisticated and regulatorily immature. This framework is not all-encompassing, nor will we touch all sub-risk categories in this paper, but it should provide the reader with a preliminary framework that can be further built upon as the industry continues to evolve. We suggest that a common consideration for a token due diligence approach is whether the code at the social layer is commonly overlooked or whether it has an immense impact on the future of the system. We also suggest that it is common to suggest that autonomous autonomous organizations (DAOs) should be considered as a potential source of value in the future.

Digital Assets Regulation: Insights from Jurisdictional Approaches is published by the World Economic Forum. The report provides a close analysis of the regulatory frameworks in nine jurisdictions. It draws key lessons from each approach and reveals the lessons that may result from the different regulatory approaches. As of early September 2024, the total market capitalization of cryptocurrencies, one type of digital asset, was valued at $2.01 trillion. There is a need for clear guidelines in the fast-olving digital assets industry, says the report. It is hoped the report will help policy-makers and regulators craft effective and harmonized frameworks that promote innovation while safeguarding stakeholders. The findings, interpretations and conclusions expressed herein are a result  of a collaborative process facilitated and endorsed by the world Economic Forum, but do not necessarily represent the views of the forum or its Members or other stakeholders.

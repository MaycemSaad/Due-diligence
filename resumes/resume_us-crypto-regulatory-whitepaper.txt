Digital assets are rife with fraud, scams and                 abuses in certain applications, says Gary Gensler, Chairman of the US Securities and Exchange Commission. The broad and widespread access and availability of digital asset platforms across the globe, coupled with patchy regulatory guidance, makes the space highly susceptible to investor abuse. This article will focus primarily on market manipulation risks witnessed within the digital asset marketplace. It will also discuss regulatory and control considerations specific to                 digital assets that organizations can deploy to effectively manage market risk. The article is scheduled to be published on December 10, 2021, at 10:30 a.m. ET (11:30 p.M. ET) and will be available for download at the following URL: http://www.cnn.com/2019/01/10/digital-asset/index.html#storylink=cpy.
